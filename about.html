OUR LITTLE CURRY STORY

Two cultures. One comfort bowl.

Good food has a way of bringing us home.

Colonel’s Curry began with a simple craving: the deep, slow-simmered comfort of Japanese curry, shared with the generous spirit of a Filipino family table.

We cook our curry fresh every day, building each pot with real ingredients, patiently layered spices, and a little love. From comforting Japanese Curry to aromatic Laksa Curry and our rotating Seasonal Curry, every bowl is made to brighten your day.
